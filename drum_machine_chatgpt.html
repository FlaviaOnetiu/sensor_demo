You don't need to be a drummer to start making beats.

🥁 Kick

The kick creates the main pulse of your beat. Try placing it on steps 1 and 9.

👏 Snare

The snare usually hits on beats 2 and 4. This gives the groove its backbeat.

🔔 Hi-Hat

Hi-hats fill the spaces between kicks and snares. Try activating every other step.
