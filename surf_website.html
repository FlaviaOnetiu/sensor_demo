🧠 Learn to Surf

Understand the basics before you paddle out.

🏄 How to Pop Up

Your pop-up should be quick and controlled.

  • Place hands beside your chest.
  • Push your body upward.
  • Bring your front foot forward.
  • Keep your knees bent.
  • Look toward the beach.

🌊 Catch a Wave

Timing is one of the most important skills.

  • Watch the wave approach.
  • Turn toward shore.
  • Paddle early.
  • Feel the board accelerate.
  • Then pop up.

💨 Understand Wind

Wind can completely change surf quality.

  • Offshore usually cleans waves up.
  • Onshore can create chop.
  • Light winds are often easier.
  • Check wind direction before paddling out.

⏱️ Understand Period

Wave period tells you how much time passes between waves in a swell.

  • Short period = closer-spaced waves.
  • Long period = more powerful swell.
  • Longer period can mean stronger waves.
  • Always consider height and conditions together.

🛟 Ocean Safety

The ocean deserves respect.

🌪️ Rip Currents

If caught in a rip, stay calm and avoid fighting directly against the current. Swim parallel to shore to escape it.

👀 Know Your Limits

Surf conditions can change quickly. Choose waves that match your experience.

🏄 Respect the Lineup

Don't drop in on another surfer. Communicate, take turns, and give others space.
